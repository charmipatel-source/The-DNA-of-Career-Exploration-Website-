THE GAP

> The bridge between students and the local biotech industry.

> Although New Jersey is a top-three biotech hub in the country, local students often lack awareness and access to the opportunities in their community.

> Many students feel pressured to choose a specific career path early without any real experience or application. Most high schools do not have modern biotech laboratories, making it difficult for students to practice hands-on skills.

OUR SOLUTION

> The “DNA Career Ladder”: an interactive digital resource designed to help students in their biotechnology career exploration.

> With focus on accessibility and local connections, our resource utilizes a four step biology themed journey to identify personal interests, train soft skills, connect with peers, and find practical experience opportunities.

> By providing actionable steps, our resource aims to give students a clear understanding of the field they will thrive in.
